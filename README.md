## 💼 Experience

**SDE II & Team Lead — Lookfinity · Idea Clan** _(Feb 2023 – Present · Panchkula, India)_
Joined the founding Python team as an IC, promoted to SDE II & Team Lead within a year. Every ad network runs on nearly the same campaign structure, but each names things differently and each is missing something the others have — so media buyers were launching platform by platform, by hand, at volume. I designed a centralized service that takes **one configuration and fans it out across every network**, scaled to **2,000 ads/minute** in production; the quarter after launch came within reach of the previous full year's volume. The Python work started because Google's Ads SDK was first-class in Python while our stack was Node: I built the company's first Python reporting service around it, which opened the door to AI creative generation conditioned on geography, keywords and psychological triggers. Also a video hashing system — near-identical creatives were crowding out the top-performer leaderboard — and an ad-library intelligence service built on public ad libraries only.

**Software Engineer (Contract) — Samsride** _(Dec 2022 – Feb 2023 · Remote)_
Full-Stack Python Developer on the Innovations Refund platform, across Django and React on a tight timeline. I took it because the problem was good, not because I needed a bridge: a centralized FinTech platform where banks aggregate and loan flows originate in one place. Centralization was the interesting part — structurally the same problem I'd go on to solve for ad networks a year later.

**Junior → Associate Software Developer — Pheuture Studio Pvt Ltd.** _(Aug 2021 – Nov 2022 · Noida, India)_
First professional role. Built WhatsApp and Shopify connectors on the Odoo platform. Promoted to Associate within 6 months — extended platform integrations, applied machine learning to product features, and deepened backend ownership. I left when the role started drifting from Python into mostly SQL work. I wanted to keep going deeper in Python, not sideways.

**Choices, not defaults** — FastAPI over Django because the architecture is microservices and FastAPI stays lightweight, and over Flask because async was non-negotiable. PostgreSQL for the reporting service, where the shape is fixed and the queries are analytical; MongoDB for the launch service, where every network's config is dynamic and half-structured; ClickHouse for ingesting high-volume reporting data. CrewAI because I can define discrete crews with assigned jobs, instead of handing one agent everything and hoping.

## 🚀 Ventures

### [Mithahara](https://mithahara.com) — Breakfast Stall _(Founded Jan 11, 2025 · On Hiatus)_

Started from zero with my wife on a roadside stall. Built to ₹100K+/month revenue, 7 dishes, 4 employees, and a 5-day work week in 6 months. Served 150 full meals at a single Yoga Day event. Customers still call to place orders. Planning a comeback and building toward a global healthy-food brand.

### Fiber Fruit Cups _(2025 · On Pause)_

Fresh fruit cups with 35g+ fiber per serving — a direct response to the fitness world's over-indexing on protein and under-indexing on fiber. Sold out at a local public event, landed corporate bulk orders. Paused due to market-education challenge; still believe in the idea.

### [ClipMark](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/clipmark/iboippnihpcnnglgboaiedaiimbiolgg) — Chrome Extension _(Idea: Late 2022 · Built: Mar 2026 · Live)_

In late 2022, deep in long YouTube tutorials as a junior engineer, I kept hitting the same wall: there was no way to save what you found. Come back after two days and you'd scroll for ten minutes to relocate a single explanation. The idea sat with me for years; in March 2026 I built it. Bookmark any YouTube moment, Revisit saved sections in sequence, Share clips, and let AI surface what matters. Built for students, content creators, and developers who rely on YouTube for learning. **[Live on the Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/clipmark/iboippnihpcnnglgboaiedaiimbiolgg)** · [clipmark.mithahara.com](https://clipmark.mithahara.com)
