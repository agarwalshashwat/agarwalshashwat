★ Best Moment

Our office placed a bulk order of ~120 cups and we sold out completely at a local football event. Proof the product worked when it found the right crowd.

After the breakfast stall went on hiatus, a close friend — a serious fitness enthusiast who had been deep in nutritional research — brought me an overlooked insight: the fitness world obsesses over protein while under-indexing on fiber, which is equally critical for digestion and making protein absorption actually work. His idea: sell fresh fruit cups packed with at least 35g of fiber per serving, making it easy for people to hit their daily fiber goals in one go.

We sourced fruits directly from the local wholesale produce market — guava, apple, green apple, dragon fruit, kiwi, and pomegranate — and assembled cups priced at ₹60–80 (~$0.70–$1). The product was genuinely good. The challenge: we were trying to educate a market that wasn’t yet asking the question. Conversion was hard. Many people hadn’t heard of a daily fiber goal, and the price felt steep without that context. We didn’t crack it this time.

I remain a strong believer in the idea. The world is moving fast toward fiber-aware, gut-health-conscious eating. We just needed to meet people where their awareness already was, rather than starting the conversation from scratch. With the right marketing approach — leading with benefits people already care about — I’d back this again.

★ The Core Idea

Bookmark the moments that matter. Hit Revisit — ClipMark walks you through every saved section in sequence. One click to get back everything you found. Share your clips with anyone.

In late 2022, deep in long YouTube tutorials as a junior engineer trying to level up fast, I kept hitting the same wall: there was no way to save what you found. Come back after two days and you’d scroll for ten minutes trying to relocate a single explanation. No bookmarks. No notes. No memory. The idea sat with me for years. In March 2026, I finally built it.

ClipMark is a Chrome extension that turns YouTube into a knowledge tool. Bookmark any moment, tag it, and come back to it with one click. Revisit Mode walks you through all your saved sections in sequence — structured review with zero scrubbing. Share your clips with teammates, students, or anyone who needs to see exactly what you found. AI layers on top to group related clips and surface what matters across a video or playlist — your YouTube brain, finally externalised.

Built for students who learn from YouTube because it’s free and want notes they can actually revise, content creators who prep by marking key moments in past interviews before recording, and developers who want to flag a specific deep-dive in a long conference talk without losing it. Free educational content is everywhere — the tooling to learn from it properly has always lagged behind.
